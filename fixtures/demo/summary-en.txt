Report from the garden group

Over the summer the garden group looked after the shared planting boxes in the courtyard. The boxes were watered every morning by a rota of about eighty volunteers, and the rota was put up by the front door in May. The tomatoes were late because of a cold early summer, but the harvest was enough for everyone who wanted some.

An open afternoon with juice and buns was held in August. About twenty neighbours came to look, and several asked how to join. The group took names and phone numbers on a sheet of paper, and the list is kept by the chair.

In the autumn the group plans to plant bulbs and clear the empty boxes. Next year it would like to plant more berry bushes along the fence, and suggestions for varieties that do well in the area are welcome. A meeting about this is held on 14 November at 6 pm in the building's common room.
