Solbacken Allotment Association — Minutes of the committee meetings, 2026

Meeting 1

Meeting 1 was held on 2026-01-12 at 18:30 in the clubhouse. Anders Holm opened the meeting and 31 members were present. The agenda was approved without changes, and Maja Berg was appointed to keep the minutes.

The first item concerned: the clubhouse roof. 4 members asked to speak. They described the present state, explained what had been done during the previous year and asked how much work remained. The chair noted that the board had inspected the matter on 2026-01-07.

The meeting decided to spend 120 EUR on this item. Maja Berg is responsible for the work and will report to the board by 2026-02-11. The treasurer, Karin Lindqvist, confirmed that the money is available in the account.

Several members asked that the work be planned so that it disturbs as few neighbours as possible. It was agreed that noisy work may only be done on weekdays between 09:00 and 17:00, and that the board will send a short notice to all 64 plot holders at least 7 days before.

Price list for the season: plot rent 450 EUR per year, water 18 EUR per month, key deposit 50 EUR, and hire of the clubhouse 60 EUR per day. The prices for the next year will be decided at the next annual meeting.

Any other business: Petra Voss asked about the notice board, and the chair promised an answer at the next meeting. The meeting was closed at 20:15.
